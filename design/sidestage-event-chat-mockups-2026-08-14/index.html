Make Event Chat useful while the room is moving.

Three high-fidelity directions replace the passive, stat-heavy rail with a seller workflow: notice the right question, understand its context, answer safely, and keep the room’s energy visible.

3 concepts Desktop + mobile Preview only
What the current rail gets wrong

The chat exists, but the seller’s next move does not.

01Too much setup chrome

Title, three stat pills, triage summary, empty state, read-only notice, and participants consume the rail before useful messages appear.

02Priorities lack actions

“Focused” changes the list, but the seller cannot reply, resolve, pin, or hand a question to Copilot from the message itself.

03Room tone disappears

Important questions and social momentum are split into competing modes, so the seller loses either urgency or the feeling of the room.
