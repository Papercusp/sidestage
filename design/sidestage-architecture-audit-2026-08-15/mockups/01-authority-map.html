What owns state?

One map, four honest statuses.

Organize SideStage by authority and label every dependency by its current runtime role. The page stops looking like a component inventory and starts answering where truth lives.

Why this wins

It preserves the complete system model while preventing configured infrastructure and available protocols from masquerading as active application paths.

Language discipline

Use “active,” “fallback,” “configured,” and “planned” as real runtime claims. Avoid “always” unless every compatibility path satisfies it.
