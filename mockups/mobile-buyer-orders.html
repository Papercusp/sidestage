On this screen

D6 The right rail becomes a sheet. Web shows "In the room", live chat and participants beside the stage. On a phone that becomes a one-line bar with the live count, opening chat as a bottom sheet. This is the only structural departure from web.
D2 Two tabs, not six. Web has Buyer, Orders, Seller, Build history, Config and Test. Mobile ships Buyer + Orders only, so the bar carries exactly two items.
— The product table stacks. Four web columns (product / price / availability / hold) become a row: thumbnail, title and brand, then price with its ready-count, and Hold on the end.
— Every empty state is kept verbatim. "No auction is live yet." and its follow-on line are the shipping copy, not rewritten.

Before the fleets build

Three calls I made that are yours to confirm

Each of these is a real fork where the web design does not dictate the phone answer. I picked one so you have something concrete to react to — say the word on any of them and I will change it before the hold lifts.

  1. Chat: a sheet, or a third tab?

    Web keeps chat permanently beside the stage. A phone cannot, so it becomes a bar that opens a sheet.

    My call: the sheet — a third tab would break the Buyer + Orders scope, and chat only matters while a room is open.

  2. Hold: inline, or inside a product sheet?

    Inline keeps buying one tap away but leaves the row tight; moving it into a tapped detail sheet gives the product room to breathe.

    My call: inline — on a live floor, speed of holding an item beats detail.

  3. The stage: inline, or full-bleed?

    Full-bleed video looks better; inline keeps the watching / sold / raised stats visible without scrolling.

    My call: inline with gutters — the stats are the reason a seller watches this screen.
